1. Open Bank Account and use it.
2. Transfer Funds to local accounts. Account number must be exactly 8 digits to work.
3. Transfer Funds to International  accounts, Account number must be exactly 12 digits to work.

5. View Available Balance
6. View Transaction History
7. View your account Number both local and international
